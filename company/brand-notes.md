# Brand Notes

> Purpose: The visual and strategic brand rules an AI or collaborator uses to keep design output on-brand.

## Brand positioning

Northstar Growth Studio should feel like an operator's desk made visible: structured, practical, calm, and built for decision-making. It should not feel like a generic SaaS landing page or a loud agency pitch. The brand is about turning scattered material into governed, repeatable systems.

## Color system

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage notes |
|------|------|-----|-------------|
| Primary | Midnight Navy | #0F1C2E | Headers, deep backgrounds, system diagrams, high-authority surfaces |
| Secondary | Signal Teal | #0E8A7C | Supporting accents, links, selected states, workflow highlights |
| Accent | Decision Amber | #E8A020 | Calls to action, key emphasis, alert-level highlights |
| Neutral / text | Ink | #1F2933 | Primary body text on light backgrounds |
| Background | Warm Paper | #F7F4EE | Page background and low-intensity content zones |
| Surface | White | #FFFFFF | Cards, forms, tables, and content containers |
| Border | Soft Stone | #D8D2C5 | Dividers and card borders |

Accessibility target: body text and interactive elements should meet WCAG AA contrast at minimum.

## Typography

- Headline: Inter or system-ui, 600-700 weight.
- Body: Inter or system-ui, 400-500 weight.
- Fallbacks: system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif.
- Heading style: sentence case, plain language, minimal punctuation.
- Body style: short paragraphs, scannable lists, clear labels.

## Logo usage

This public-safe demo does not include production logo binaries. Logo references are captured in `fonts-logos-assets/manifest.csv` so downstream systems know what to request or verify.

Rules:

- Use the primary logo on light surfaces.
- Use reversed or mono variants only when listed in the asset manifest.
- Do not redraw, recolor, stretch, or invent logo variants.
- If the asset is missing, mark it as a gap rather than substituting a generic mark.

## Imagery and photography style

Use calm, professional, documentary-style imagery: workspaces, notes, dashboards, planning surfaces, interface details, and real humans collaborating without stock-photo exaggeration.

Avoid:

- handshakes
- generic skyscraper business imagery
- neon AI brains
- crowded collage layouts
- overused startup stock photos

## Iconography

- Style: simple line icons.
- Stroke: consistent 1.5-2px equivalent.
- Corners: slightly rounded.
- Use icons for scanning and meaning, not decoration.
- Avoid mixed icon packs.

## Layout and spacing principles

- Use an 8px-based spacing rhythm.
- Favor editorial clarity over dense dashboard compression.
- Every section should have a clear purpose: orient, explain, compare, prove, or convert.
- Cards should group decisions or concepts, not merely decorate content.
- Forms should explain what happens after submission.

## Brand don'ts

- Do not make unsupported performance claims.
- Do not sound like a generic automation platform.
- Do not use jargon where plain English works.
- Do not add visual noise to prove sophistication.
- Do not invent missing assets, fonts, logos, metrics, or customers.
