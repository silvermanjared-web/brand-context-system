Northstar Growth Studio is a fictional public-safe B2B brand used to demonstrate how brand context becomes design-system structure.

## Elevator blurb

Northstar Growth Studio helps marketing teams turn scattered context into usable operating systems. The sample brand exists to demonstrate how voice, visual direction, code references, asset manifests, Figma notes, and review rules can be packaged for consistent design-system extraction and front-end handoff.
