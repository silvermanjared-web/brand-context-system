## Conversion patterns to catalog

- **Above the fold**: headline + subhead + single primary CTA + proof.
- **Form design**: field count, progressive disclosure, inline validation.
- **Social proof placement**: where testimonials/stats appear relative to the CTA.
- **Objection handling**: FAQ, guarantees, risk reversal.
- **CTA repetition**: how often and where the primary action repeats.
